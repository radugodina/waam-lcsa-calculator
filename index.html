Methodology and scope

The interface is recreated from the published graphical design, shared Google Apps Script modules and the actual Google Sheets equations. Calculations run locally in your browser without a server, making this version deployable as a static site on GitHub Pages.

Life Cycle Assessment (LCA)

Cradle-to-gate inventory of HSLA steel wire, electricity, 82% Ar / 18% CO₂ shielding gas and CNC finishing. Single scores in ReCiPe eco-points; detailed midpoint factors are included for benchmarking.

Life Cycle Costing (LCC)

Machine depreciation, maintenance, tooling, steel feedstock, electricity, shielding gas, finish machining and labour. Cost assumptions are editable to reflect a particular facility.

Traditional CNC benchmark

Cylindrical or cuboidal feedstock; subtractive machining time estimated using feed rate × width × depth of cut. Steel bar material, machining electricity, machine and operator costs are compared.

Core computational equations

Build rate (cm³/min)BR = π × wireDiameter² × wireFeedSpeed / 4
WAAM deposition & coolingt_dep = (V + S × a / 10) / (BR × 60)
t_cool = ceil(10 × h / layerHeight) × t_layer / 3600
Wire & shielding gas consumptionm_wire = 7.8 × (V + S × a / 10) / 1000
gas_m³ = gasFlow_Lmin × t_dep × 60 / 1000
Single-score LCA (Pts)SEI = (m_wire × CF_wire + gas_m³ × CF_gas + E_WAAM × CF_electricity + E_finishing × CF_electricity) / 1000
Machine hourly charge (€ / h)(assetCost + depreciationYears × annualMaintenanceAndTooling) / (depreciationYears × workingDays × shiftHours × shifts × availability)
Cost per part (€)C = C_machine + C_material + C_electricity + C_gas + C_finishing + C_labour

Model fidelity & limitations

This version reconstructs the parametric equations visible in the connected workbook, including its workbook-specific finishing energy approximation and treatment of cooling time. It does not execute the Google Sheets custom-menu Apps Script. The source contains inconsistencies between some legacy formulas, printed paper equations and cell labels; these are documented rather than hidden. The social screening assessment reproduces the accessible Sheet2 binary criteria; Monte Carlo uncertainty is implemented as a separate browser simulation rather than an exact reproduction of Google Sheets RAND() draws. Original chart mappings have been reconstructed from the provided Apps Script services and view templates. Country reference data are historical and should be updated before research or policy use.

The paper's scope is HSLA steel, with raw material and manufacturing phases only. Results should not be presented as verified product declarations without checking electricity-mix datasets, characterization factors, tool-path-specific energy, asset utilization, data provenance and sensitivity.
